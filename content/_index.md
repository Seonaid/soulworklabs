Whether they are too big or too small, too early or too late, awkward or terrifying, **we set our callings down for years at a time in pursuit of something more practical**. 

We earn money, raise families, solve problems, and become good representatives of whatever groups we belong to. We hide behind identities that are easy to explain, walk paths that are well-defined, and do work that "makes sense."

But there's a voice inside that whispers, "That's not me. I know what I really am." It says, "Pay attention to this thing over here, this thing that seems pointless. This is what really matters."

<!--col2-->

These are what I mean when I say, "Callings": these esoteric desires for something specific, regardless of its practical import or transactional value on the open market - **these are the universe noticing something about itself**. Through you.

Whatever it is that animates you - the local manifestation of the universe, the soul, atman, the self-aware pattern that has developed using your DNA and your experiences as its raw materials - cares about this thing. 

It resents the demands that pull it away, leaving the thing unnoticed, unremarked: The poem unwritten, the cat unpetted, the *huge project* unresourced.

And it comes back again and again, asking you to find something out.
