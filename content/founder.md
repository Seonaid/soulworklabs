Soulwork Labs is a consolidation of work that has been spread across sites and conversations for the last thirty years. On one level it is a report on the application of reflective and embodiment practices to my own life (with instructions on how to replicate the bits that have worked over time). On another, it is a work of practical philosophy.

I have spent my life *obsessed* with moral reasoning, especially in technical professionals. I studied it in school. I wrote about it. I enticed people at technical conferences to talk about ethics over coffee. But my day-to-day never turned into a related role, even while it was obviously needed.

Instead I spent years on other things: caring for children and community, building things, using my skills of perception to document systems and provide guidance. Some of it was well-compensated and came with prestige. Much of it was unpaid and done on the invisible margins. And through it all, I wrestled with questions much larger than offered by my immediate world, up to and including, "What does the universe want from me?"

Sometimes the answer was very weird.

<!--rest-->

> It may be that when we no longer know what to do, we have come to our real work — and when we no longer know which way to go, we have begun our real journey.
>
> — Wendell Berry

Even after all these years, I wish I had been content with a conventional, well-paid job with clear boundaries... the type that’s easy to explain at dinner parties. I "should" have been able to do that, as I am "good at" a lot of things. 

**But I *burn* for deep reality — and I'm here to invite you to join me on that quest.**

What I trust is the felt sense of alignment — moving *with* what we have perceived rather than against it. I trust that we can tell the difference between the discomfort of the ego and the yearning that brings us closer to the living world and the consciousnesses that we share it with.

This is a lab, not a temple. Everything here is provisional and experimental by design. It is also grounded in thousands of years of other people's teaching. This is not, "I tried this and it worked," but rather, "I tried all of these things and I'm pretty sure that this one worked because of this other thing that is usually considered something else entirely, but YMMV, let's give it a shot."

If you’ve had real, uncanny, hard-to-explain experiences but stayed skeptical of the systems that rush to explain them... welcome. I have some things that might help.

I don't know what happens next. But nobody else does either.
